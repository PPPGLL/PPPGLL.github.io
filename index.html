GaolePan

I study how materials look, and how to render them.

Ph.D. student at Nanjing University, advised by Prof. Beibei Wang.

Most of my work starts from a surface that existing models get wrong. Lately that means porous materials that change as they soak up water, light that bounces several times between microfacets before it leaves, and recovering a surface's microstructure from images of it.

Research

  • Wet material rendering

    Porous materials look different once water gets into them: darker, more saturated, glossier. I build appearance models that follow that change.

  • Microstructure reconstruction

    Working backwards from images to the small-scale geometry of a surface, and to the statistics that describe it.

  • Physically based rendering

    Light transport in materials with complicated internal structure, including the multiple bounces that simpler models leave out.

Projects

  • Interactive WebGL2

    Competition projectInteractive demo

    Bubble Rendering Demo

    Soap bubbles you can play with in the browser. Thin-film interference follows Belcour's model, with HDR reflection and transmission. Bubbles wobble, collide, share a film where they touch, and build up into foam. Ported to WebGL2 from the original HarmonyOS/OpenGL ES version.
